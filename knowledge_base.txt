
FOODRESCUE AI - KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT FOODRESCUE AI:
FoodRescue AI is a food donation and food sharing platform.
It connects people who have surplus food with people and organizations that need food.
Its main goal is to reduce food waste and help people in need.

FOOD DONATION:
Donors can share surplus food through the Donate Food page.
They should provide their name, food name, category, quantity, preparation time, collection deadline, storage information, location and contact details.
Donors should provide accurate information about the food.

FIND AVAILABLE FOOD:
Users can visit the Find Food page to view available food donations.
They can check the food name, quantity, category, location and other donation details.
Users can select a suitable donation and submit a food request.

FOOD REQUESTS:
People and organizations can request available food.
They should provide their name, organization name, contact number, requested quantity and purpose.
The donor can approve or reject the request.
A submitted request remains pending until the donor takes action.

SMART MATCHING:
FoodRescue AI includes a Smart Matching feature.
Users enter their required food category, quantity and location.
The system searches for suitable donations.
It calculates a match score to help identify suitable food donations.

FOOD SAFETY:
Food should be stored properly and handled hygienically.
Donors should provide accurate preparation and expiry or collection deadline information.
Food that appears spoiled, contaminated or unsafe should not be donated.
Recipients should check food condition before consumption.
Perishable food should be transported and collected promptly.

REDUCING FOOD WASTE:
Food waste can be reduced by donating surplus food, planning meals, storing food properly and sharing extra food with others.
Food donation helps communities and reduces unnecessary waste.

RESCUEBOT:
RescueBot is the AI assistant of FoodRescue AI.
It helps users understand food donation, food requests, available food, smart matching, food safety and food waste reduction.
It should answer politely using simple language.
